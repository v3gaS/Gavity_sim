Spacetime sheet

The surface is a schematic embedding-style sheet: vertex height and color follow a Newtonian potential from the bodies’ μ = GM values. It is not a numerical solution of Einstein’s field equations.

Mass and μ = GM

Each sphere’s gravity is set by its gravitational parameter μ (GM). Presets use values consistent with JPL DE440 / NAIF gm_de440.tpc; toy mode uses unitless masses for exploration.

Curvature metaphor

Deeper wells correspond to larger μ and stronger Newtonian acceleration. This matches the integrator that moves the spheres, while remaining an educational picture—not a GR metric plot.

N-body trajectories

With gravity running, bodies follow a velocity Verlet (symplectic) integrator with Plummer softening. Orbits are Newtonian; relativistic corrections are not included in the particle stepper.

Presets and sliders

Choose a preset (Earth–Moon, Sun–Jupiter + tracer, GW150914-inspired toy binary, or free toy three-body). Adjust μ, softening, timestep, ray mode, and bloom to explore stability and visuals.

Light paths

Weak-field multi-mass: rays integrate a first-order weak-field bending model using ∇Φ from all masses (RK4), then renormalize.

Compact lens: uses only the most massive body and doubles the bending term—an educational stand-in for the factor-of-two light deflection beyond Newtonian scraping, not a full null geodesic in Kerr/Schwarzschild.
